# Crystal Spa — Design System
Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Design a world-class luxury booking platform for **Crystal Spa – Suite Spa Privative**.

The website should immediately communicate:

- Luxury
- Exclusivity
- Romance
- Wellness
- Trust
- Premium Hospitality

Think:

- Airbnb Luxe
- Aman Resorts
- Four Seasons
- Ritz Carlton
- Apple-level minimalism

NOT a traditional hotel website.

The experience should feel modern, immersive and premium.

---

# Design Philosophy

Less but Better.

Large images.

Generous whitespace.

Smooth animations.

Elegant typography.

Beautiful cards.

Every interaction should feel premium.
